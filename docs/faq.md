# TechMart FAQ Knowledge Base

## Returns & Refunds

**Q: What is your return policy for electronics?**
Our return policy allows returns within 30 days of purchase with the original receipt
and original packaging. Electronics must be in resalable condition. Refunds are
processed within 5-7 business days to the original payment method. Items that have
been opened must include all original accessories and documentation.

**Q: Can I return a final-sale item?**
Final-sale items are non-returnable under our standard policy. However, if the item
arrives damaged or defective, you are eligible for a full refund or exchange regardless
of the final-sale designation. Contact support within 48 hours of delivery with photos
of the damage.

**Q: How long does a refund take to appear?**
Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive and inspect the
returned item. Depending on your bank or card issuer, it may take an additional 3-5
business days to appear on your statement.

**Q: What if I lost my receipt?**
A receipt or proof of purchase is required for all returns. If you purchased online,
your order confirmation email serves as your receipt. In-store purchases without a
receipt may be eligible for store credit at the current selling price.

## Shipping & Delivery

**Q: How long does standard shipping take?**
Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days from the date of dispatch. Expedited
shipping (2-3 business days) is available at checkout for an additional fee.
We do not offer guaranteed overnight or same-day delivery on electronics.

**Q: Do you ship internationally?**
We currently ship to the contiguous United States only. Alaska, Hawaii, and U.S.
territories are served at standard rates with an additional 2-3 business days added
to the estimated delivery window.

**Q: What happens if my order is delayed?**
If your order has not arrived within the estimated delivery window, contact our
support team with your order number. For verified delays caused by our logistics
partners, we will issue a shipping credit of $10 to your account.

**Q: Can I change my shipping address after placing an order?**
Address changes can be made within 1 hour of order placement by contacting support.
Once the order enters the dispatch queue, the shipping address cannot be modified.

## Products & Warranty

**Q: What warranty is included with electronics?**
All electronics sold by TechMart include a minimum 1-year manufacturer warranty
covering defects in materials and workmanship. Extended warranty plans (2 or 3 years)
are available at the time of purchase and can be added within 30 days of the sale date.

**Q: How do I register a warranty?**
Warranty registration is completed at the manufacturer's website using the serial
number on the product packaging. TechMart cannot register warranties on behalf of
customers. Failure to register does not void the manufacturer warranty.

**Q: What is not covered under warranty?**
Accidental damage, liquid damage, cosmetic damage, and wear from normal use are not
covered. Damage caused by unauthorised modifications or repairs voids the manufacturer
warranty entirely.

## Orders & Payments

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, and TechMart store
credit. We do not accept cryptocurrency, personal cheques, or cash on delivery.

**Q: Can I cancel my order?**
Orders can be cancelled within 1 hour of placement at no charge. After 1 hour,
cancellation requests are not guaranteed. Once an item has shipped, the standard
return policy applies.

**Q: Is buy-now-pay-later available?**
We offer instalment plans through our financing partner for purchases over $200.
Annual Percentage Rate (APR) ranges from 0% to 29.99% depending on credit approval.
The APR, total amount financed, and payment schedule will be disclosed before you
confirm financing. All financing terms comply with the Truth in Lending Act (TILA).

## Account & Privacy

**Q: How do I reset my password?**
Visit the login page and click "Forgot password". Enter your registered email address
and you will receive a reset link within 5 minutes. The link expires after 30 minutes.
If you do not receive the email, check your spam folder or contact support.

**Q: How is my personal data used?**
We collect only the data necessary to process your orders and improve your shopping
experience. We do not sell personal data to third parties. Full details are in our
Privacy Policy. Payment card details are never stored on our servers — all transactions
are processed by our PCI-DSS-compliant payment partner.

**Q: How do I delete my account?**
Submit a deletion request through Account Settings > Privacy > Delete Account. Data
is purged within 30 days except where retention is required by law (e.g., tax records
are retained for 7 years per IRS requirements).
